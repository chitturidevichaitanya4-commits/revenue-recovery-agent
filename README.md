**# Revenue Recovery Agent**



**An AI-powered revenue recovery system that detects failed payments, prioritizes recovery cases, selects intelligent recovery actions, and tracks recovered revenue through a feedback loop.**



**## 🚀 What Problem Does It Solve?**



**Failed payments can cause merchants to lose revenue and customers to churn.**



**The Revenue Recovery Agent analyzes failed payment events and decides:**



**- How important is this recovery case?**

**- What recovery action should be taken?**

**- How much revenue is potentially recoverable?**

**- What is the probability of successful recovery?**

**- What happened after the recovery attempt?**
